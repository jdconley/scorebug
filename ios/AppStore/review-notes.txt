Scorebug films a volleyball match on an iPhone, keeps score, and streams the video to the team's YouTube channel with the score drawn on the video.

SIGNING IN
Tap "Sign in with Google" and use any Google account. No demo account is needed: every account can create a team. Scorebug uses Google sign-in because its core feature streams to the user's own YouTube channel, which needs their Google account; Sign in with Apple cannot grant access to a YouTube channel. Scorebug sees only the name and email, and asks for YouTube access separately, only when a team owner connects a channel.

TRYING IT WITHOUT A YOUTUBE CHANNEL
YouTube only lets channels with live streaming turned on go live, and turning it on can take YouTube up to 24 hours. To see everything else:
1. Sign in, tap Create team and name it.
2. On the home screen tap New match, type an opponent name, and choose "Record only, don't stream".
3. Turn the phone to landscape. Tap the left or right half of the screen to score points; Undo and Swap sides are at the bottom. At 25 points Scorebug asks before ending the set.
4. Tap Invite scorer to see the QR code and match code a second phone uses to keep score (on a second device: Join with a code, no account needed).
5. Tap End to finish; the recording is saved under Recordings on the home screen.

WITH A YOUTUBE CHANNEL
On the team screen tap "Connect a YouTube channel" and approve access, then New match and Go Live. The broadcast is unlisted; Watch link shares it.

APPLE WATCH
With a match open on the iPhone, the Watch app shows the score; tap a team to add a point, and confirm set ends from the wrist.

DELETING AN ACCOUNT
Settings (gear on the home screen) > Delete account deletes the account and its data on our server.
